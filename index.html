How to Play

Use the arrow keys to navigate through the maze. Collect points and reach the exit to advance to the next level.

When you need an advantage, sacrifice a key to gain special abilities. Once sacrificed, that key can no longer be used!

Each level features a more complex maze. How far can you go with fewer controls?
