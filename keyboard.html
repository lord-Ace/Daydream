How to Play

Use the arrow keys to move your character and avoid obstacles. Collect points to increase your score.

When you need an advantage, sacrifice a key to gain special abilities. Once sacrificed, that key can no longer be used!

Each level gets progressively harder. How long can you survive with fewer controls?
